What is Breadwire and what is my vision?
Breadwire is a browser-based electronics workbench that bridges the critical gap between software simulation and physical hardware assembly. It allows students to design schematics, simulate digital logic (from truth tables to 74xx chips), and automatically translate them into 2D/3D breadboard routings.

The Mission (Social Impact):
My goal with Breadwire is to democratize hardware education. Universities and schools in Latin America often lack the budget for expensive proprietary software (like Proteus or Altium). I want to provide free "Education Licenses" to public universities and high schools, allowing students to learn electronics without the barrier of costly software.

The Open-Core Strategy:
To ensure Breadwire's financial sustainability while keeping the social impact alive, we use an Open-Core model:

The Core (Open Source): The foundational schematic translator and basic 2D/3D UI will remain open-source and free for everyone.

The Pro (Commercial): Advanced features like the High-Level Synthesis (HLS) engine to generate VHDL/Verilog code for FPGAs, and institutional dashboards for professors, will be commercialized to fund the free licenses for schools.
